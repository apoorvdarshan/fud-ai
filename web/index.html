Do I need a subscription?

No. Manual logging, tracking, and using your own AI key don’t require a Fud AI subscription. Hosted Plus and Pro are optional paid plans.

Does my whole diary go to an AI provider?

No automatic diary upload happens. What is sent depends on the action you choose. Food analysis can send your selected photos or description, and Coach can request relevant history for an answer. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I use it without an internet connection?

You can use local features such as manual logging and viewing your diary offline. Remote AI and barcode lookups need a connection. Supported on-device models can handle certain tasks once downloaded.

Can I see my totals without opening the app?

Yes. Add a home-screen widget on iOS or Android. The Apple Watch app shows nutrition totals and can log water when enabled; supported watch faces can show nutrition complications.

What can I use without an AI key?

Manual logging, barcode lookup, food and workout history, water, and fasting don't need an AI key. For supported devices and tasks, you can also use an on-device model.

How does the fasting timer work?

You choose when to start, stop, or edit a fast. Fud AI doesn’t infer fasting from a skipped meal or recommend how long to fast.

Is there an Android APK?

Google Play is the usual install. Signed Android release files and verification instructions are linked from GitHub Releases.

Is Fud AI the same as the Fud app?

No. Fud AI is the open-source nutrition and workout tracker by Apoorv Darshan. Use the App Store and Google Play links on this site to find the correct app.

How does Fud AI compare with Cal AI?

Both can estimate nutrition from meal photos. Fud AI is open source and keeps your diary on your device by default. See the Fud AI vs Cal AI comparison for differences in subscriptions, AI setup, and privacy.

Where can I report a bug?

Open a GitHub issue or use /bug in the Fud AI Discord. Reports submitted through the bot become public GitHub issues, so don’t include private information.
