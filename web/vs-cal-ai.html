Fud AI vs Cal AI

Independent comparison · Updated November 26, 2026

Both Fud AI and Cal AI can estimate calories and macros from a meal photo. Fud AI is an open-source Cal AI alternative with a diary stored on your device by default. Manual logging and barcode lookup work without a subscription or AI key. For AI estimates, bring your own key or choose optional paid Hosted AI.

Which app fits your routine?

Choose Fud AI if you want to log without a subscription, keep the diary local by default, or inspect the source code. A hosted scanner may be simpler if you don’t want to compare models or manage a key. Fud AI’s Hosted Plus and Pro are the paid, no-key option.

For a packaged lunch, Fud AI can look up the barcode without an AI key. For homemade food, scan the meal photo with a configured model, then check the portion and edit the estimate. Neither app’s photo estimate substitutes for a nutrition label or professional advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fud AI a free alternative to Cal AI?

Fud AI is free to install on both platforms. Manual entry, barcode lookup, and tracking do not require a subscription. Fud AI does not charge you to use your own AI key, but the provider may set limits or charge for usage. See what’s free and what needs an AI key.

Do I need an API key for photo scanning?

Cloud AI in Bring Your Own Key mode needs a compatible key. Other options include a compatible local model with Ollama, on-device photo analysis on eligible iPhones, or an optional paid Hosted AI plan. No key is needed for barcode lookup or manual entry.

Where does my food data go with Fud AI?

Fud AI keeps the diary on your device by default. BYOK sends what the requested feature needs directly to your provider; Hosted AI routes eligible requests through Fud AI’s service. The Privacy Policy details other optional features, such as backup and meal sharing.
