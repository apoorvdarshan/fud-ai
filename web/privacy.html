Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026
Hosted AI and billing Live Voice Meal sharing Delete your data

Fud AI is an open-source iOS and Android app developed by Apoorv Darshan. The core tracker has no required account, in-app analytics, behavioral tracking, general Fud AI cloud sync, or ads. Your diary is stored locally. Data can leave the app for specific features such as AI processing, health integrations, sharing, optional backup, and the separately opt-in Challenge boards. Paid Hosted Plus/Pro is optional; you may keep using your own API key. The homepage voice example uses fictional data and does not request microphone access or send any input.

Discord support

For community moderation, Fuddy also checks new messages in accessible server text channels and active threads approximately once a minute. Deterministic spam rules run on Cloudflare; moderation messages are not sent to an AI provider. We store Discord identifiers, message fingerprints, strike counts and timestamps in a private Durable Object, but do not persist message text there. Member records are eligible for deletion after 30 days without a scanned message; strikes reset after 30 days without a new strike. Warnings appear in the affected channel. Moderation outcomes and uncertain-promotion flags, including identifiers and message links, appear in a private staff log and remain in Discord until removed. Automatic timeouts and third-strike bans follow the server rules. Contact support to appeal or request removal of moderation records.

When you submit /ask, /bug, or /feature, our Discord bot processes your command through Cloudflare. Questions and reports may be sent to Google Gemini. Bug reports and feature requests become public GitHub issues; do not include credentials, private logs, or personal information. New reports do not add your Discord username or ID to GitHub. To notify you when an issue closes, we privately store its number and your Discord user, channel, and bot-response IDs for up to two years. The bot replies in the original channel and mentions you with the closure outcome, which does not imply an app release. Unsent notifications expire after seven days; closure deduplication records expire after two years. Request removal of a report's notification mapping through our support contact.

How We Use Your Information

  • AI Food Analysis: When you choose to analyze a meal, the app processes the selected food photos, notes, or description, including descriptions produced from voice input. Multiple images can be sent separately in the same request. In Bring Your Own Key mode, requests go directly to the provider or endpoint you configure. A configured fallback can receive the request if the first attempt fails. OpenRouter’s openrouter/free option lets OpenRouter choose the upstream provider. Custom AI Instructions stay local except when included in an AI request. Manual logging does not require AI processing.
  • On-device AI: Supported devices can process eligible photos or food descriptions locally using downloadable Gemma models. Supported iPhones can use Apple Intelligence as the final fallback for eligible food descriptions after BYOK attempts fail, or for eligible photo analysis on iOS 27 when selected; a configured remote fallback can receive the request if local photo analysis fails. Downloading a model requires a network request but does not send the meal you later analyze.
  • Optional Hosted AI (iOS and Android): If you subscribe and select Hosted AI, the Cloudflare Worker receives the payload for your request, which can include prompts, images, requested Coach context, or audio. It validates your RevenueCat entitlement, meters usage in a hashed ledger, and forwards the relevant payload to the upstream service. It does not receive your BYOK keys or an automatic copy of your diary. New subscriptions are paid with no free trial.
  • iOS Siri Shortcuts / App Intents: If you use Siri to log food, the spoken food description is handled as text input and sent through your selected AI access path to estimate nutrition; on supported iPhones it may use Apple Intelligence locally only as the final fallback after BYOK provider/fallback attempts fail. The resulting entry is logged locally and written to Apple Health if Health access allows nutrition writes. If you use Siri to log weight, the app parses the number locally, respects pounds/kilograms in the phrase or your unit setting, stores the weight locally, and writes it to Apple Health if authorized. If you ask Siri for today's calories, the app reads today's local totals and calorie goal. Phrase examples are shown under + → Describe Meal → Siri Phrases.
  • Meal What-If Suggestions: If you tap What if? on the Review Food screen, the app locally previews the meal's calorie/protein/carbs/fat impact. If it asks AI for a suggestion, it sends the reviewed meal name and macros, your current daily totals, daily goals, and minimal profile context through your selected AI access path. The meal is not logged until you tap Log.
  • Barcode Lookup: On iOS and Android, if you choose Barcode from the logging menu, the scanned barcode is sent from your device to the public Open Food Facts API. Fud AI requests only product fields needed for nutrition logging, such as product name, brand, quantity, serving size, and nutriments. If Open Food Facts has no product or incomplete nutrition, the app reuses a nutrition label you previously scanned for that barcode, which stays on your device. Optional USDA FoodData Central (Settings → Product Information): only if you add your own free FoodData Central API key and neither Open Food Facts nor a saved label has the product, the app sends that barcode (in its common zero-padded forms) directly from your device to the USDA FoodData Central API to look up a branded food with the same GTIN/UPC. Your key is sent only in a request header, never in the URL; nothing is sent to USDA without a key, and removing the key stops these requests. If no source has the product, the app reports that it could not be used; you can instead photograph the packaging with Camera or Photos. Barcode lookup does not use your AI provider key.
  • AI Coach Chat: When you use the Coach tab, the app sends a slim system prompt (your profile, BMR/TDEE/macro formulas, weight forecast, current date/timezone, and a one-line "data available" snapshot) plus your conversation history through your selected AI access path. The snapshot can indicate how many explicitly recorded fasting sessions are available. Coach can additionally call tool functions mid-turn to fetch relevant ranges of your weight, body-fat, calorie, food-entry, fasting-session, workout-plan, workout-preference, or completed-workout history when you ask about specific dates, fasting, or training. If you attach an image to a Coach message, that image is included in the AI request. The fetched data is sent as part of the AI request and may include meal nutrients, explicitly recorded fasting start/end/goal data, or workout exercises, sets, reps, weight, RPE, and calculated calorie burn when relevant. Coach does not infer fasting from a gap in your food log. Full chat history is kept locally; only the last 20 messages are included in each LLM request to cap token cost.
  • Optional Nutrient Goal Estimation: If you tap the AI estimate button for detailed nutrient goals, the app sends the minimum profile context needed for that estimate, such as your goal, body measurements, activity level, and current calorie/macro targets, through your selected AI access path. Returned detailed goals such as fiber, sugar, fats, cholesterol, sodium, potassium, calcium, iron, magnesium, zinc, vitamins, folate, omega-3, and related nutrients are stored locally as settings and do not change the calorie/protein/carbs/fat calculator.
  • Speech-to-Text: If you pick the on-device option (Native iOS on iPhone, Native Android SpeechRecognizer on Android), voice recognition happens on your device and the selected language is passed as a local recognition hint when applicable. On Android, if the native recognizer reports that the selected language is unsupported or unavailable on-device, the app may retry native Android recognition with network/provider defaults before showing an error. In BYOK mode, if you pick a remote provider (Gemini Audio, OpenAI Whisper, Groq, Deepgram, AssemblyAI), a short audio recording is uploaded directly from your device to that provider and its text response is returned to the app. Groq (Whisper) and Deepgram are recommended alternatives when native Android speech is inconsistent on a specific phone. If you choose a fixed speech language or "Use Device Language" instead of Provider Auto, the corresponding language code may be sent with the audio so it can bias transcription.
  • Local Storage: All food entries, Siri-created food entries, saved Review Food nutrition edits, weight entries, Siri-created weight entries, body-fat readings, optional body-circumference measurements (waist, hips, neck, chest, arms, thigh, calf, wrist) you choose to enter, optional water entries and goals, explicitly started fasting sessions and fasting settings, workout plans and logged sets, workout preferences, calculated workout-burn history, user profile (including any goal body fat %), Coach chat history, food photos, cached thumbnails, optional nutrient goals, Adaptive Goals preferences, Home nutrient-card choices, meal-time boundaries, and app preferences are stored in the app's local container — primarily UserDefaults on iOS and DataStore Preferences on Android. Body-fat-aware protein target display, water progress, fasting elapsed time, and workout-burn estimates are calculated locally from that data. Fasting sessions use a separate local model and never alter calories, macros, or meal grouping. Data leaves that container only through operating-system backup/transfer behavior or the specific requests and sharing actions described in this policy.
  • Optional Weekly Challenge: The challenge is off until you choose to join, confirm that you are 18 or older, and accept its community rules. The app creates a random participant ID and one-time bearer credential; the credential is kept in secure device storage and the service stores only its SHA-256 hash. Cloudflare D1 receives only your chosen display name, optional X or Instagram handle, acceptance timestamps/version, weekly aggregate metrics, and service timestamps. It does not receive your birth date, detailed food or workout logs, photos, body measurements, API keys, Coach history, fasting/water history, or camera frames. Challenge names, optional handles, aggregate scores, ranks, and stable participant IDs are visible only to other enrolled participants with a valid challenge credential, not to anonymous website visitors or search engines. A social handle may let other participants identify or contact you on that external service, so adding one is optional. Authenticated participants can report another participant; reports store the reporter and target IDs, a selected reason, optional details of up to 300 characters, moderation status, and timestamps. We use focused display-name filtering, participant reports, and transient rate-limit counters to protect the community; raw bearer tokens and IP addresses are not written to the challenge database or application logs.
  • API Key Storage: On iOS, your API keys (including an optional USDA FoodData Central key) are stored in iOS Keychain using the app's private Keychain service and become available after the device is first unlocked. On Android, they are stored in EncryptedSharedPreferences using AES-256 with a master key managed by Android Keystore (hardware-backed when the device supports it).
  • Widgets and Apple Watch: To display today's calorie, selected nutrient, and optional water totals in iOS Home Screen widgets (Fud AI, Fud AI Protein, and Fud AI Water), iOS Lock Screen widgets, Android Glance widgets (Calorie, Protein, Today, and Water), and Apple Watch, the main app writes a small snapshot of the relevant totals and goals into a shared container — App Group on iOS, the same DataStore on Android. iOS also sends a small nutrition snapshot to the paired Watch app through WatchConnectivity when available. These snapshots never leave your devices and contain only totals, goals, nutrient labels, theme choice, and whether water tracking is enabled — no food names, photos, account identifiers, or API keys.
  • Water Tracking and Reminders: Water tracking is off by default. Water amounts, goals, and progress are stored and calculated locally, and reminders are local notifications. On Android, you can separately enable water sync and grant the Health Connect Hydration write permission. Fud AI then writes the amount and time of logged water changes and can delete its corresponding Health Connect records when you remove an entry while water sync is enabled. It does not read water history from Health Connect. Turning water sync off stops further sync; it does not erase existing Health Connect samples.
  • Fasting Tracking and Goal Alerts: Fasting tracking is off by default. If you enable it, the app stores only sessions you explicitly start, end, cancel, edit, or delete, plus your freely chosen 1–168 hour goal. The active timer is derived locally from its saved start time and continues across app restarts until you end or cancel it. An optional one-time local notification can alert you when the goal is reached; there is no permanent timer notification, and the fast continues until you end it. Disabling fasting cancels an active session but preserves completed history. Fasting data is not sent to Apple Health or Health Connect and is included in an AI request only through Coach as described above.
  • Optional iCloud / Google Drive Backup: Off until you turn it on in Settings → Data Management. iCloud on iPhone or Google Drive app-data access on Android is used only when you turn it on there, or when you choose Restore from iCloud or Restore from Google Drive on the Welcome back screen while setting up the app — never automatically at install. A restore turns backup on again with the restored settings. The app then uploads a snapshot of your food log, saved meals, meal photos, weight, body fat, measurements, water, fasting, workouts, goals, preferences, and Coach history to your iCloud or Google Drive. API keys, Weekly Challenge credentials, and device-specific Health tokens are not uploaded. On Android, workouts imported from Health Connect are not uploaded either; they are imported again from Health Connect on the new phone. Restore keeps original entry IDs so Apple Health / Health Connect do not double-write. You can back up, restore, or delete the cloud file from the same Settings card. iCloud backups and Drive backups are not interchangeable. Fud AI still has no account of its own.
  • Operating-System Backups and Device Transfer: Apart from the optional Weekly Challenge's aggregate fields and the optional iCloud / Google Drive backup above, Fud AI does not run its own cloud-sync service. Apple and Android may nevertheless include eligible app-container data in encrypted device backups, Google backup, or direct device transfer according to your platform and account settings. The iOS Keychain accessibility class used by Fud AI also allows saved keys to migrate with an encrypted backup. These copies are controlled by Apple or Google, not by Fud AI. Delete All Data clears the current app installation but cannot rewrite an older platform backup that already exists.
  • Exports and Shared Meal Links: Diary exports are generated locally and leave the app only when you save or share the file. See Optional Meal Sharing for the difference between Short and Direct links and what their recipients can see.
  • Apple Health (iOS) / Health Connect (Android): With your permission, Fud AI can write per-meal nutrition, body measurements where supported, and calculated workout active calories. Workout plans, set details, and fasting sessions are not written. Android water sync requires the separate setting and permission described above. Fud AI can read authorized steps, body measurements, and energy data for progress or goals. On iOS, authorized profile imports can also include height, date of birth, and biological sex. Apple Health workouts and, on Android, sessions from other apps with the optional Exercise permission can be imported as read-only history. This may include type, start/end or duration, distance where available, active calories, and source. Health Connect imports are kept on the device, omitted from Google Drive backup, and removed from the app the next time you open it after revoking Exercise access. Imported workouts are not written back. Where supported, Fud AI can backfill authorized samples and read back the nutrition, weight, body-fat, and workout-energy records it previously wrote. This does not restore Fud AI’s full water or fasting diary or workout plans and sets. Review or revoke permissions under Manage Access or directly in Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Notifications: If you enable streak, daily-summary, log-weight, log-body-fat, meal, water, or fasting-goal notifications, they are scheduled locally via iOS local notifications or Android alarm/notification services plus the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission on Android 13+. Metric-aware reminders can skip on days you have already logged the corresponding item. Enabling fasting on Android creates its dedicated notification category; disabling it removes that category and cancels a pending fasting-goal alert. No notification content is sent to a Fud AI server.
  • App Update Checks: Fud AI can check when it opens or returns to the foreground, and when you request a check under About → App & Updates. iOS requests public App Store metadata for Fud AI; Android asks the Play In-App Update API whether an update is available for your installation. These checks do not send your diary, profile, keys, or health data. If an update is available, tapping Update opens the store listing.
  • Website Hosting and Assets: Cloudflare serves the website, the optional challenge and Hosted AI services, and short meal links. It may process standard request metadata to deliver and protect them. Some guide and legal pages request fonts or icons from Google Fonts or cdnjs. The homepage demo uses local assets and fixed examples, with no analytics or microphone access. Shared-meal preview pages use a no-referrer policy.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not use any in-app third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • We do not track your behavior, location, or browsing activity
  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We do not operate general tracker accounts, in-app analytics, or behavioral-tracking servers; the optional challenge uses a pseudonymous participant credential only
  • We do not require an account, sign-in, or cloud sync for the core tracker; optional meal sharing and Weekly Challenge send only the fields described above

Supported on-device analysis does not upload its input, but a configured remote fallback can receive the request if on-device analysis fails.

Tips & Hosted Plans (App Store and Google Play)

The iOS and Android apps offer optional Tip Jar purchases that support development and unlock nothing, plus optional Plus/Pro subscriptions and consumable credit packs for hosted AI convenience. Payment is processed entirely by Apple under your Apple ID or by Google Play under your Google account; we never see your payment details. The apps use RevenueCat to fetch products, validate purchases, and identify the anonymous subscriber used for hosted quota — RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and the App Store receipt or Google Play purchase token, never your food, health, or profile data. Hosted quota and credit balances are stored as a hashed ledger on Cloudflare D1 and verified against RevenueCat on the server. In Hosted AI mode, the photos, text, audio, and Coach messages of each request you make are forwarded through Fud AI's server to Gemini or Deepgram and are not stored by Fud AI.

Voice assistant

The optional voice assistant is a live spoken conversation that you start and can end at any time. While a session is open, your voice audio, the live captions of what you and the assistant say, camera frames while you have the assistant's camera open, and the results of the in-app actions it performs for you (for example a draft meal's items and nutrition, or totals it reads to answer you) are streamed to the provider you chose: Google (Gemini Live) when you use Fud AI Plus/Pro hosting, or Google Gemini, OpenAI, xAI, or Alibaba Qwen (Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, international region) directly from your device when you use your own API key. Hosted sessions pass through Fud AI's Cloudflare Worker, which relays them without storing their content and records voice allowance and credit use in a hashed usage ledger. Random billing receipts also record the charge source and timestamp and are removed after 30 days; they contain no conversation content. Resumption handles are authenticated for your subscriber identity, expire after two hours, and are not stored or logged by Fud AI. Deleting anything always needs your confirmation, and finished turns are added to your on-device Coach history only when Save conversations to Coach is on. The chosen provider's privacy policy applies to what it receives.

Optional Meal Sharing

The first time you share a meal, the app asks which link to send, and you can change it later in Settings → App Settings. With Short Link (the default) while online, the app sends the selected meals (names, nutrition, ingredients, serving details, barcode product details, and any custom notes; no photos, API keys, or profile) to Cloudflare Workers KV to create a short link. Links expire automatically after approximately seven days. Anyone with the link, including a messaging service generating a preview, can read and copy the shared meals. Expiry does not remove copies already imported or saved by recipients or messaging services. Cloudflare processes request metadata for delivery and rate limiting. If creation fails or you are offline, sharing falls back to the existing link containing the meal payload. With Direct Link, the app sends nothing to Fud AI: the meals are carried in the link itself, which opens Fud AI directly and does not expire. Share pages use a no-referrer policy.

Barcode/Product Data Provider

Barcode lookup uses the public Open Food Facts API. When you scan a barcode, the barcode value is sent to Open Food Facts so the app can retrieve public product and nutrition data when available. Open Food Facts' own policies apply to that lookup.

USDA FoodData Central is an optional second source that is off by default. Only when you add your own free FoodData Central API key, and Open Food Facts and your saved labels have no nutrition for a scanned barcode, the barcode value is sent with your key to the USDA FoodData Central API (operated by the U.S. Department of Agriculture through api.data.gov). USDA's own policies apply to that lookup and to the key you registered with them.

Permissions Requested

  • Camera — for snapping food photos, scanning barcodes, and showing food to the voice assistant. Prompted on first use.
  • Microphone / Speech Recognition — for voice meal input and the voice assistant. Prompted on first use.
  • Photo Library / Storage — to pick existing food photos. iOS uses PHPicker; Android uses the system PickVisualMedia photo picker — neither requires broad photo-library access.
  • Notifications — only if you enable meal, streak, daily-summary, weight, body-fat, water, or fasting-goal notifications. On Android 13+, this triggers the runtime POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission prompt.
  • Health — only if you enable Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android) integration. The system sheet lists the requested read/write types. Energy reads are used for goal calculation only when Energy Burn Goals is enabled; active-energy writes are used only when you calculate and sync a workout burn. On Android, the Exercise read permission is optional and is used only to show workouts that other apps shared with Health Connect, read-only.
  • Internet — required for AI/STT requests to your chosen providers, optional app-update checks, and the optional Weekly Challenge when you join or view it. Implicit on iOS; declared in AndroidManifest.xml on Android.
  • Battery Optimization (Android, optional) — Settings exposes a deep-link to whitelist Fud AI in Android's battery-optimization list. This is needed on aggressive OEM skins (OriginOS, MIUI/HyperOS, One UI) where exact alarms are killed otherwise. Whitelisting only affects local reminder reliability — it does not change anything about data handling.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • Short meal-share payloads expire from Cloudflare Workers KV after approximately seven days; recipients may retain their own copies.
  • Local app data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or remove the app, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior.
  • Weekly Challenge scores are retained for 13 challenge weeks. Daily scores are retained for 35 date keys and Monthly scores for 13 calendar months. Daily and Monthly are separately opt-in. Each shares the period start, aggregate habit counts including balanced days and longest streak, overall points, rules acceptance timestamp/version and score timestamps; neither shares diary entries or calorie quantities. Leaving a Daily or Monthly board removes all scores for that board type. Reports are retained for up to 180 days for moderation. An active challenge profile remains until you leave; profiles inactive for 90 days are automatically deleted, cascading their remaining scores and reports.
  • Choosing Leave Challenge sends an authenticated remote-deletion request. Delete All Data also requests challenge deletion when an enrollment exists and the service is reachable. If a reinstall or lost device destroys the only bearer credential before deletion can be requested, the 90-day inactivity rule provides automatic cleanup; you may also contact us with a privacy request.
  • Delete All Data (Settings → Delete All Data) wipes the app's local storage on both platforms: food log, weight log, body-fat log, water log, fasting sessions, workout diary/plans/history, profile, Coach chat history, saved meals, optional nutrient goals, Home nutrient-card choices, widget / Watch snapshot, API keys, and preferences. It intentionally does not touch Apple Health or Health Connect — those samples are personal and survive the reset. If you want them removed too, on iOS go to Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Fud AI; on Android use Health Connect's data management screen.
  • Clear Food Log (Settings → Clear Food Log) removes local food entries only; previously-synced Apple Health / Health Connect samples are kept.
  • Uninstalling removes the app sandbox, but Apple Health / Health Connect samples persist unless deleted separately. On iOS, Keychain items can also survive an uninstall by platform design; use Delete All Data before uninstalling if you want Fud AI to explicitly remove its saved API keys.

Children's Privacy

Fud AI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The optional Weekly Challenge is restricted to people who confirm they are 18 or older; we store that confirmation timestamp/version and never send or store the locally held date of birth for this purpose. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can assist.
